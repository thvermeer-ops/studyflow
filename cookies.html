Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how StudyRise uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our app.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use localStorage, which is a similar technology that stores data in your browser.

2. How We Use Storage

StudyRise primarily uses localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your data locally on your device. This includes:

Key Purpose Type
sf_tasks Your homework tasks Essential
sf_grades Your grade data Essential
sf_pro_email Your Pro subscription email Essential
sf_theme Your dark/light mode preference Preference
sf_streak Your study streak data Functional
sf_badges Your earned badges Functional
sf_settings Your app settings Preference
sf_tour_done Whether you've seen the onboarding tour Functional
sf_trial_start Your free trial start date Essential

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you sign in with Google, Google may set cookies on your device as part of their authentication process. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

When you make a payment, Stripe may set cookies as part of their payment processing. Please refer to Stripe's Privacy Policy for more information.

4. Essential vs Optional Storage

Essential storage is required for the app to function. You cannot opt out of this without stopping your use of StudyRise.

Preference and functional storage improves your experience but is not strictly necessary.

5. Managing Your Data

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Going to Settings → Data → Clear All Tasks in the app
  • Clearing your browser's localStorage via browser settings
  • Using your browser's developer tools

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at: privacy@StudyRise.app
