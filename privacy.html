Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

At StudyRise, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our homework planning app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use StudyRise:

  • Google Account information — your name and email address when you sign in with Google
  • Usage data — tasks, grades, and study data you enter into the app
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we never store your payment details
  • Technical data — browser type, device type, and usage patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the StudyRise service
  • Verify your Pro subscription status
  • Send you important notifications about your tasks and deadlines
  • Improve the app based on usage patterns

3. Data Storage

Your task and grade data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Your account information is stored securely in our Supabase database. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth — for secure sign-in
  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Supabase — for secure data storage
  • Anthropic Claude API — for AI study tips (Pro feature)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (e.g., payment processing with Stripe).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data at any time via the Settings page
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

7. Children's Privacy

StudyRise is designed for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies and localStorage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. Please see our Cookie Policy for more details.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via email or a notice in the app.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@StudyRise.app
